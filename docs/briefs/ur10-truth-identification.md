# Experiment brief: UR10 / simulated truth fixture / dynamic identification (D7)

Instance of the [experiment brief template](../experiment-brief-template.md).
Evidence is in the [reference workflow](../development/identification-reference-workflow.md);
this brief records the decisions and what you may conclude from them.

## Outcome and scope

- Application and quantity to improve: joint-effort prediction (N·m) of a
  UR10 arm model, and a URDF whose link inertials carry the identified
  values. Reference workflow for the method, not a statement about a physical
  UR10.
- Task: dynamic identification.
- Supported scope and explicitly excluded effects/tasks: rigid-body inertial
  parameters from simulated effort of six revolute joints. Excluded: friction,
  actuator inertia and joint offsets (the truth has none), any real UR10,
  motor current conversion, payload changes, joint elasticity, calibration.
- Core/examples revisions and linked issue: examples#23 (reference
  workflow), #21 (fixture). Accepted on core `658fe99` + examples `663d642`;
  re-run here on core `779e830` + examples `b167298`.
- Acceptance criteria and their justification: for each joint, held-out
  RMSE <= 1.25 * sigma_low + 0.01 N·m, with sigma_low the effort noise the
  fixture injected. This is a noise-floor bound: a model that predicts as
  well as the noise allows passes. It was set from the protocol, not tuned to
  the fit.

## Unsupported assumptions and limits

- The result says nothing about a physical UR10: the truth is the same model
  class the fit uses, so errors from unmodelled effects cannot appear.
- The held-out limit is a noise-floor bound; a pass means the estimator is
  as good as the noise permits on this motion, not that the model generalises
  to other motions or payloads.
- Friction is not modelled because the truth has none; on real data it
  would have to be.
- Individual masses, first moments and inertias are not identified; only
  base combinations and the feasibility-completed vector.
- The stated limits are tied to `--noise low` and the frozen seeds; other
  noise levels or seeds have no declared limit.
- Offline logs only; no deployment claim (`claim` in `reproduction.json`).
